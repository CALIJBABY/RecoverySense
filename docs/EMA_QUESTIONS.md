# EMA Question Bank

These are starter questions. Arman/clinical advisor should revise them.

## Craving questions

1. What are your cravings right now?
2. How strong is your craving from 0 to 10?
3. How confident are you that you can resist the craving right now?
4. Did something trigger this feeling?

## Context questions

1. Where are you right now?
2. Are you alone or with others?
3. What were you doing in the last 10 minutes?
4. Are you feeling stressed, bored, anxious, or excited?

## Sensor-triggered examples

- High heart rate while low movement: ask about stress or craving.
- High movement plus high heart rate: ask about exercise or environment.
- Low sleep score next day: ask about fatigue and cravings.
