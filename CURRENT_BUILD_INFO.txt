Current participant-facing state:

- Live sensor data and sleep context are separate screens.

- Craving Risk Now displays only a recent, approved, sensor-window model probability; it never copies the latest EMA rating into the gauge.
- The actual selected model name is shown. Random forest is displayed only when the persisted model provenance says random_forest.
- Three top positive local explanation patterns are sorted and refreshed from each new approved prediction.
- EMA remains the repeated 0–10 outcome label used for model training and validation and is shown separately as the latest check-in.

Important interpretation limits:
- When the selected model is not the decision tree, the three displayed patterns come from the companion decision tree trained on the same feature set; they are not exact random-forest weights.
- Displayed patterns are predictive associations, not causes.
- The craving model remains a research framework until trained and validated on real participant EMA-labeled data.
- Sleep output remains an estimated binary sleep/wake context, not validated staging or diagnosis.
- Raw Samsung PPG remains disabled.
- OS-backed scheduled EMA delivery after app termination remains a pre-field blocker.
